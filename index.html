our story

Born in Cape Town. Built for the universe.

Hover a year to trace the story.

Saturn started in Cape Town as W3O LABS, built on one bet. Blockchain could solve real problems in Africa. It did. We shipped tools people actually used and proved the thesis on the ground.

The bet paid off. We backed and built alongside 140+ startups across the continent, learning firsthand what founders here need to get off the ground and stay there.

One frontier was never going to be enough. We opened NOD3, a home for the next wave of builders, and carried the model into LATAM. AI, fintech, stablecoins, the openings kept multiplying, and so did the founders chasing them.

W3O LABS was built for one world. Saturn is built for the universe. We now run three verticals. Saturn ventures backs founders, Saturn ecosystem advises corporates and institutions, and Saturn foundation grows the next generation of African entrepreneurs.
