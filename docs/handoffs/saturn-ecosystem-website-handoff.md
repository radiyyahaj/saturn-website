# Saturn Ecosystem — Website Handoff

**Purpose**  
This document contains the approved content, structure, tone, and interaction notes for the Saturn Ecosystem marketing website homepage. Use it as the single source of truth for development.

**Brand positioning**  
Saturn Ecosystem is a boutique technology services firm (positioned as a focused, high-calibre alternative to larger players like Entelect).  
Tone should feel corporate, professional, confident and steady — not startup-flashy, not overly sharp or “deck-like”.

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure (Homepage)

Keep the page deliberately simple and uncluttered:

1. Hero
2. Services (with hover expansion)
3. Clients / Logos
4. Final CTA / Contact

Do **not** include:
- Positioning matrix
- “How we work” section
- Culture section
- Domains strip
- Selected Work / Case studies (none available yet)

---

## 2. Tone of Voice

- Corporate volume (closer to Entelect than to sharp startup copy)
- Confident, pragmatic, delivery-oriented
- Clear and professional
- Outcome-focused without hype
- Short-to-medium length sentences
- Avoid jargon stacking and marketing fluff

Preferred energy: steady, experienced, reliable.

#### Service Cards (Hover Interaction)

Each card has two states:

- **Default state**: Title + short one-line teaser (visible on load)
- **Hover / expanded state**: Longer description appears as the card grows

**Interaction notes**  
- On hover (or focus), the card should expand smoothly and reveal the longer description.
- Keep the expansion elegant and controlled — not dramatic.
- Ensure the layout remains stable and does not cause major page reflow.
- Mobile: consider tap-to-expand or always show the fuller description in a clean stacked layout.

---

**Card 1 — Custom Software**  
Default: Enterprise applications, platforms and integrations.  
Hover: We design and build robust systems that support core business processes — from customer-facing platforms and internal tools to complex integrations across existing systems. Built to be maintainable and ready to evolve as the organisation grows.

**Card 2 — AI & Automation**  
Default: Intelligent systems that reach production.  
Hover: We develop AI and automation solutions that move beyond experimental stages into live environments. These systems support decision-making, streamline operations and become part of the day-to-day running of the business.

**Card 3 — Data & Analytics**  
Default: Turning data into clearer decisions.  
Hover: We help organisations make better use of their data through business intelligence, data platforms and practical analytics. The focus is on insight that leadership and teams can actually act on — whether that’s operational visibility, customer understanding or forecasting.

**Card 4 — Payments & Infrastructure**  
Default: Reliable systems at the core of the business.  
Hover: We build and modernise payment systems and core operational infrastructure. These are the foundational platforms that need to be secure, stable and able to scale as transaction volumes and business complexity increase.

**Card 5 — Cloud & Security**  
Default: Practical cloud and security foundations.  
Hover: We support organisations with cloud architecture, migration and modernisation, alongside the security and controls required to operate with confidence. The emphasis is on practical implementations that reduce risk and improve operational resilience.

**Card 6 — Strategy & Roadmaps**  
Default: Clear plans ready for delivery.  
Hover: We work with leadership teams to identify high-value opportunities, define the right architecture and sequence the work. The result is a clear roadmap and business case that a delivery team can pick up and execute with confidence.

## 4. Design & Interaction Notes for Claude Code

- Aesthetic and branding assets already exist (supplied separately by the user). Respect the existing visual identity.
- Keep the overall feel calm, corporate and spacious.
- Services section is the commercial heart of the page — give the cards good breathing room.
- Hover expansion on service cards should feel refined and controlled.
- Prefer clean typography hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Primary CTA style should feel confident and clear.
- Ensure excellent mobile responsiveness (especially the service cards).
