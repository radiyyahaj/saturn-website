## 3. Global Design & Interaction Guidelines

- **Tone of language:** Speedrun-inspired — short, direct, confident, benefit-first. No long corporate paragraphs.
- **Colour:** Purple is the primary accent (buttons, highlights, key borders, active states).
- **Typography:** Strong headlines, generous spacing, high readability.
- **Cards:** Use for differentiators, metrics, and quotes.
- **Buttons:** “Apply now” must appear in the header, hero, and final CTA.
- **Mobile:** Everything must stack cleanly. CTAs must remain highly visible.
- **Overall feel:** Modern accelerator energy with corporate restraint. Clean, not flashy.

---

## 4. Content Principles for Claude

- Keep sentences short.
- Prefer clear statements over explanations.
- Do not add extra marketing fluff.
- Do not revert to dense “deck” language.
- Maintain consistency with the rest of the Saturn website voice, but lean into the more direct Speedrun register for this page.
