the foundation

What is Saturn foundation?

Saturn foundation is a South African nonprofit company (NPC) built to make entrepreneurship a practical, scalable route to a more equal economy, so that young founders who have already started can build businesses that last, and the work those businesses create reaches beyond the founder.

the program

What Startup School is

A six month online program for people who have already started and are ready to grow. The first cohort begins January 2027.

You get weekly teaching on the live business, and on the founder running it, in a cohort of eight to fifteen. Teachers are founders and owners who have built and sold real companies. At month six a panel awards three to five grants of R100,000. Everyone who finishes receives twelve months of mentorship.

what you get

Six months of school, a grant earned at demo day, and a year of mentorship.

Skills

Commercial skills from week one

Pricing, sales, cash flow and go to market, applied to your business as you learn them.

Coaching

Weekly sessions with founders who have built and sold real companies

The business and the person. Numbers, customers and the next decision, and the mindset, energy and judgement underneath them.

Funding

A R100,000 grant, earned at demo day

Three to five grants per cohort. No equity. No repayment. The founder keeps the company.

Network

Twelve months of mentorship for everyone who finishes

Monthly check ins, buyer introductions, and a cohort that stays together after school ends.

who should apply

Who we are looking for

Founders aged 18 to 35, based in South Africa, who have already started. Early users or early revenue, in any sector.

  • A product, service or prototype that already exists
  • Early users or early revenue
  • Aged 18 to 35 and based in South Africa
  • Ready to be taught weekly for six months

For example

  • A software product with early users
  • A hospitality business with returning customers
  • A consumer product with online sales
  • A services business with paying clients
  • A shop or side business with regulars
